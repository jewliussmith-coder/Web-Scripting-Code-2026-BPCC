West Wall

Morning

The sun comes up,
The sky turns bright,
A brand new day
Begins with light.

Rain

Drip drop falls,
Across the ground,
Tiny silver drops
Make a gentle sound.

East Wall

Stars

Stars above,
Shining bright,
Little sparks
Across the night.

Ocean

Waves roll in,
Waves roll out,
The ocean sings
Without a doubt.
